TradeSketch Estimator: Offline Blueprint-First Material Takeoffs

Build faster, cleaner estimates for drywall, concrete, gravel/mulch, and paint work. TradeSketch is designed for contractors, estimators, and serious DIY users who need practical quantity numbers without cloud accounts or subscriptions.

FEATURES:
- Blueprint-first workflow for walls, openings, room surfaces, measured arcs, and sketch curves
- Trade-specific takeoff modes: drywall, concrete, gravel/mulch, paint
- Materials and quantities views with adjustable waste and pricing inputs
- Smart warnings to catch low/high waste and margin assumptions
- Export options: estimate PDF/text, blueprint PNG/PDF, CSV, JSON
- Business identity fields for branded estimate output

BUILT FOR:
- Drywall crews and remodelers
- Concrete and sitework estimators
- General contractors and project managers
- Skilled trades teams needing fast field-side estimates

OFFLINE + PRIVACY:
- No account required
- No ads or tracking SDKs
- No internet permission required
- Data stays local to your app install

OUTPUT YOU CAN USE:
- Drywall: sheet counts, screws, mud, totals
- Concrete: slab-based yardage and pricing
- Gravel/Mulch: yards/tons with density controls
- Paint: wall-area-based gallons and pricing

IMPORTANT:
TradeSketch provides estimate-only planning values. Always verify field dimensions, site conditions, and supplier pricing before purchase or construction.
