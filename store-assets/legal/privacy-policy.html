Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 9, 2026

Summary: TradeSketch Estimator respects your privacy. This app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information stays on your device.

1. Information Collection

TradeSketch Estimator is designed as a completely offline, privacy-first application. We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information or usage data.

2. Data Storage

All project data, measurements, and estimates you create are stored locally on your device only. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not transmitted to any servers
  • Is not accessible to us or any third parties
  • Can be deleted by uninstalling the app (in-app data)
  • User-exported files saved to shared folders (for example Downloads) may remain until you delete them manually

3. No Tracking or Analytics

TradeSketch Estimator does not use:

  • Analytics services
  • Crash reporting
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media integrations
  • User accounts or authentication

4. Permissions

The app uses minimal permissions:

  • Storage Access Framework: Used only when you explicitly choose to export files (PDF, CSV, JSON, or PNG). You control exactly which files are created and where they are saved.

The app does NOT request permission for:

  • Camera or photos
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Microphone
  • Phone or SMS

5. Third-Party Services

TradeSketch Estimator does not integrate with or share data with any third-party services.

6. Children's Privacy

This app does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Data Security

Since all data stays on your device, security is managed by your device's operating system. We recommend:

  • Keeping your device updated with latest security patches
  • Using device encryption (enabled by default on modern Android devices)
  • Using a secure screen lock

8. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our privacy practices, we will update this policy and the "Last Updated" date. Any changes will be reflected in app updates.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at:

Email: built.to.cell@gmail.com

10. Your Rights

Since we don't collect any data:

  • There is no data to access, modify, or delete on our end
  • You have complete control over your project data on your device
  • Uninstalling the app removes all app data from your device
